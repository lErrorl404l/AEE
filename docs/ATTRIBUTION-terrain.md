# Terrain symbol attribution

The AEE terrain and map-feature symbol textures are real drawings from open
sources. AEE does not hand-draw a terrain symbol.

## DGIWG Symbol Register

The DGIWG Symbol Register graphics are used under Creative Commons
Attribution 2.0.

> Contains DGIWG Symbol Register data, (C) DGIWG, CC BY 2.0.

- Source: `https://portal.dgiwg.org/public_dgiwg/portrayal/`
- Licence: CC BY 2.0, (C) Copyright DGIWG, some rights reserved.
  Evidence: `https://github.com/DGIWG/dgiwg_wms_1.3/blob/master/license.md`
- The register is the live successor to STANAG 3675. The STANAG document
  itself is distribution-controlled and is not shipped.
- The DGIWG graphics are the primary source. They cover the works, the
  transport points, the relief and the vegetation symbols.

## FM 21-31 and the USGS sheet

The US Army field manual FM 21-31 "Topographic Symbols" (19 June 1961) and the
USGS "Topographic Map Symbols" sheet (2005) are US Government works. US
Government works are not subject to copyright in the United States
(17 USC 105). They are used as the public-domain fallback.

- FM 21-31: `https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/policy/army/fm/21-31/`
- USGS: `https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/TopographicMapSymbols/`

## Per-symbol source

The per-symbol source, concept and licence are recorded in
`data/symbology/terrain_sources.json`. Each entry names its source and its
licence.
