- Drop: max(relative * |drop|, 0.03 mrad * range); the relative floor ramps from 1 percent at 300 m to 2 percent at 1000 m.
- Wind drift: max(0.03 * |drift|, 5 mm).
- Time of flight: 1 % relative, reported only.
