PTRD – 41

CODE 03-125-941

NAME – PTRD – 41

NAME (NATIVE) – Protivotankovoye Ruzhye Degtyarova obr. 1941 g

COMMON NAMES – PTRD-41

COUNTRY OF ORIGIN – USSR

DATE OF MANUFACTURE – 1941

CALIBER – 14.5×114 mm

OVERALL LENGTH – 200.9 cm (79.1 in)

BARREL LENGTH – 135 cm (53.14 in) including muzzle brake

RIFLING (TYPE & TWIST) – 8-Grooves, Right hand twist, 1 turn in 45.5 cm (17.91 in)

LOAD – Full Metal jacketed Armor Piercing Incendiary

BULLET DIAMETER – 14.88 in (0.586 in)

BULLET WEIGHT – 63.44 g (979 grains)

MUZZLE VELOCITY – 1012 m/s (3320 fps)

MUZZLE ENERGY – 32,492 j (23,965 ft/lb)

WEIGHT (EMPTY) – 15.75 kg (34 lb 11.6 oz) without bipod

WEIGHT (LOADED) – 17.5 kg kg (38 lb 9.3 oz) with one round B-32 API and Bipod

TYPE OF MOUNT – Attached bipod

MOUNT WEIGHT – 1.55 kg (3 lb 6.7 oz)

SIGHTS – Open, Iron, Adjustable, Front sight – Blade, Rear sight – Square notch on leaf. One leaf for less than 400 Meters (438 yards), other leaf for more than 400 meters (438 yards)

EFFECTIVE RANGE – 300 meters (328 yards) against vehicles, 800 meters (875 yards) against buildings or bunkers

OPERATION – Recoil assisted Bolt action

TYPE OF FIRE – Manual

RATE OF FIRE – 8 to 10 rpm

FEED DEVICE – Single round

FEED DEVICE WEIGHT (LOADED) – 0.20 kg (7.05 oz) Single round B-32 API

BASIC AMMUNITION LOAD – Twenty rounds

LOAD WEIGHT – 4 kg (8 lb 13.1 oz)

MANUFACTURER – Soviet State Arsenals

STATUS – Obsolete

SERVICE – Soviet military, Post-war some Soviet satellite countries, notable North Korea and Communist China, and is still in use with some ex-Soviet client irregular forces.
