General Dynamics Ordnance and Tactical Systems has been producing 30mm ammunition for over 40 years. Our experience in
design, development and manufacturing of these cartridges establishes General Dynamics as a globally recognized supplier of high
quality ammunition. The 30mm x 173mm family includes armor-piercing, anti-personnel, airburst and low cost training ammunition
providing enhanced accuracy, penetration and lethality necessary to defeat a spectrum of threats on the battlefield.


MK264 MPLD-T

- Available with or without self-destruct

MK258 APFSDS-T

- Swimmer version also available
