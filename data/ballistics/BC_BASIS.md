## The independent sources

- `apg_litz_g7`: Aberdeen Proving Ground and Litz G7 ballistic coefficient list. Edition 2012-11-06.
  G7 and G8 coefficient list compiled from Aberdeen Proving Ground data, Shooting Lab conversions and the work of Brian Litz. The compiling page states the three origins and does not separate them per row.
- `litz_dfrl`: Comparing Advertised Ballistic Coefficients with Independent Measurements. Edition USAFA DFRL research report, 17-01-2012.
  US Air Force Academy research report that compares advertised coefficients against measured ones, published as DTIC ADA554683. The measured column is a Doppler-radar measurement.

## Reach of the independent basis

280 projectiles carry a maker coefficient and no measured G7 coefficient. They are the commercial hunting and match bullets. No held measurement covers them.

## Searches for a further independent source

- Searched DTIC and the Internet Archive DTIC mirror, US Army free-flight and spark-range reports. Held reports such as ARL-TR-5182 (Silton and Howell, May 2010, ADA530895, M855) tabulate the zero-yaw axial force coefficient against Mach, not a ballistic coefficient. A coefficient would have to be derived by fitting the measured curve to the G7 standard, so the value would be derived, not measured. Not entered.
- Searched Applied Ballistics, Ballistic Performance of Rifle Bullets, and the Applied Ballistics software library. Copyrighted commercial work. The library held at version 925 is an index with no coefficients. The measured coefficients are the author's paid tables, so they are not redistributed and the basis is not raised from them.
- Searched NATO RTO and AGARD technical reports. No report found that tabulates measured projectile drag for small-arms bullets. The AGARD flight-test series covers weapon delivery, not bullet coefficients.
- Searched Open-access journals and conference proceedings. Found suppressor and interior-ballistics studies, no tabulated measured coefficient for a bullet this corpus holds.
